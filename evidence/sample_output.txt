Sample calculations (regular rate card, January)
Input: 10 L, fat 3.5%, SNF 8.5%
Expected rate: Rs 30.00/litre
Expected payment: Rs 300.00

Input: 8.5 L, fat 4.2%, SNF 8.7%
Fat steps = 7; SNF steps = 2
Rate = 30.00 + (7 x 0.40) + (2 x 0.30) = Rs 33.40
Payment = 8.5 x 33.40 = Rs 283.90

Input: 12 L, fat 3.1%, SNF 8.3%
Fat steps = -4; SNF steps = -2
Rate = 30.00 - 1.60 - 0.60 = Rs 27.80
Payment = 12 x 27.80 = Rs 333.60
